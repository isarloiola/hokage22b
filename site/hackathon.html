The Ecosystem Problem

Barriers to Pi Commerce
  • ❌ Pioneers lack a public storefront: After migrating to Mainnet, merchants have no standardized way to showcase goods and their public Pi key.
  • ❌ Web2 tools take huge cuts: Platforms like Linktree, Shopify, and Stripe charge monthly fees and up to 30% cuts in fiat currency.
  • ❌ Manual billing causes errors: Copying 56-character Stellar addresses manually often leads to lost transfers or clipping attacks.

Security & Architecture

Built with Zero-Trust Web3 Principles

Engineered for security, decentralization, and high concurrency on Pi Mainnet:

Client Layer:

• Official Pi SDK v2.0 (`https://sdk.minepi.com/pi-sdk.js`)
• Zero client-side private keys or passphrases
• Responsive Webview for Pi Browser iOS & Android

Server Layer:

• Edge Functions validating Bearer tokens on `https://api.minepi.com/v2/me`
• Direct payments approval on `/v2/payments/{id}/approve`
• Automated handling of `onIncompletePaymentFound`

Database & RLS:

• PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) strictly enforced
• Direct anonymous table writes blocked by default
• Regex cryptographic validation for Stellar keys (`^G[A-Z2-7]{55}$`)
