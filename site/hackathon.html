Security & Architecture

Built on Zero-Trust Web3 Principles

No anonymous writes, no TXID replay, and server-side verification for every Pi action:

Client Layer

• Official Pi SDK v2.0 (sdk.minepi.com)
• Zero private keys or passphrases ever requested
• Strict CSP & anti-XSS escaping across cards

Edge Functions (Server)

• pi-profile, pi-payment & mural-action
• Verifies Bearer tokens on /v2/me & payments on /v2/payments
• Enforces 24h edit window, 15s comment cooldown & auto-moderation

PostgreSQL & RLS

• Zero-Trust RLS: direct anon writes revoked
• Unique partial index on payment_txid (anti-replay)
• Stellar Ed25519 regex check (^G[A-Z2-7]{55}$)
